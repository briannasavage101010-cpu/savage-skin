Questions, answered.

Everything about the Lip Pod, preordering, and what happens next. Still stuck? Email hello@savageskincare.com.

When does Drop 01 drop?

Our estimate is early 2027 — an estimate, not a promise. You can preorder at $24 (it's $32 at launch) and refund anytime before it ships, or join the community free and decide later.

What is the list?

It's the Savage Skin community — the people who get it first. Members are first to know when a drop lands, get 48-hour early access, and vote on what we make next. Joining is free — just add your email on the home page.

What exactly do I get in the box?

One Lip Pod case, one full-size gloss tube, and one refill tube — two tubes in total, so you don't run out the week it lands. After that, refills come in packs of three: $11 while preorders are open, $15 after launch.

Is the Lip Pod actually made yet?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. It's in design right now — every image on this site is a concept render, and the final Pod may differ. No manufacturer is locked in yet; that happens once the design is final. Estimated shipping is early 2027, and if that moves we email you and you can take a full refund instead of waiting.

What's in the gloss?

Clean, plant-based ingredients — shea butter, squalane, glycerin and vitamin E as the base — sourced organic where we can get it. No fragrance, no parabens, no mystery filler. The full list goes on the label in plain English, and the first batch's third-party test results go on the site, whatever they say.

Is it safe if I have sensitive skin?

It's a plain moisturizing gloss, not an active treatment — no acids, no retinoids, no vitamin C. Still, patch-test before first use if you have sensitive skin or a known allergy, and stop if it irritates. If you're unsure, check with a doctor first. Results vary, and this isn't medical advice.

Do I pay for shipping?

Not right now. Shipping is free on every preorder, with no minimum spend — a limited-time offer while preorders are open. It normally costs $4.90, so with $8 off the price you save $12.90 against the $32-plus-shipping launch price.

What's your shipping and return policy?

See our Shipping & Returns page for timelines and how to start a return. Full legal terms are linked in the footer.

Are you cruelty-free?

Yes — we don't test on animals.
