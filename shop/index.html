/ 02 — What's inside

The science.

The Pod is the idea, but the gloss is what actually sits on your lips all day. So it's clean and plant-based, organic where we can source it, and every ingredient goes on the label in plain English. No fragrance, no parabens, no mystery filler. Results vary.

/ 01
Shea Butter
Plant-based. Seals in moisture and softens — the body of the gloss.
Plant
/ 02
Squalane
Plant-derived, weightless moisture. Sits light instead of gluey.
Plant
/ 03
Glycerin
Pulls water into the lip and holds it there. All-day soft.
Plant
/ 04
Vitamin E
Natural antioxidant. Keeps the oils in the formula from going off.
