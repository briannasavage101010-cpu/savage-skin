What is the Lip Pod?

The Lip Pod by Savage Skincare is a clean, plant-based lip gloss in a refillable case that clips to your phone, bag or wrist so you never lose it.

Where does your lip gloss actually go?

Nobody loses their phone. Everybody loses their gloss. It isn’t you: gloss is designed to live loose in a bag, and loose things disappear.

  • The bag black hole

    You know it’s in there. You’ve had your whole arm in there. It is not in there.

  • The jacket you switched

    It’s in the pocket of the coat you stopped wearing when it got warm. You’ll find it in October.

  • The borrow that never came back

    “I’ll give it back at lunch.” That was two weeks ago and she’s moved on with her life.

  • The one you re-bought

    So you bought it again. And again. Same tube, same shade, three separate times.

You didn’t need a better formula. You needed one that stays attached to you.

How does the Lip Pod work?

A refillable gloss tube snaps magnetically into a small case, and the case clips to your phone, your bag or your wrist.

See every detail on the Lip Pod product page

How does it clip on?

The case hangs from a clip and cord, so it rides on the outside of your phone, your bag or your wrist instead of sinking to the bottom of something. You reach for it without looking, the way you reach for your keys.

Is it refillable?

Yes. When the gloss runs out you swap the tube, not the whole Pod, and every Pod comes with a refill in the box. Refills are the same clean, plant-based gloss.

How do the two halves stay together?

The tube snaps back into the case magnetically, so the gloss can’t wander off from the thing holding it.

Is the Lip Pod gloss clean?

It’s made to be: a plant-based gloss built from ingredients you can pronounce, every one of them listed in plain English.

Nothing in it you’d be afraid to swallow. You lick your lips, you eat, you talk, and it’s on you all day. So what goes in it isn’t an afterthought.
