/ real questions

Before you ask.

What's the difference between preordering and joining?

Preordering means you pay $18 today and we send you a Lip Pod when the first run is made. Joining the community is free — no card, no order — and just puts you on the list. Preorder if you want the Pod and the $18 price. Join if you want to watch first and decide later.

Am I being charged?

If you preorder, yes — $18 goes through today, on a secure Stripe checkout. If you only join the community, no, and there's no card field on that form at all.

Is the Lip Pod actually real?

It's real, and it's not finished. Every photo on this site is a concept render, the design is still being tooled, and the vote above genuinely decides parts of it. We'd rather show you the unfinished version than a fake finished one — which is also why the preorder terms say all of this out loud.

When does it ship?

The Pod is in design right now — we haven’t locked in a manufacturer yet, that comes once the design is final. Our estimate is that shipping could begin in early 2027. That's an estimate, not a promise — it depends on tooling and the factory. If it slips, we email everyone who preordered and you can take a full refund instead of waiting.

What if I change my mind?

Email hello@savageskincare.com any time before your Pod ships and we refund you in full. You don't have to give a reason and we won't try to talk you out of it.

Why is it $18 now and $24 later?

Because preorders fund the first production run, and money now is worth more to us than money later. That's the whole trade — you take the risk that it's early, we take $6 off.

Do I have to give my phone number?

No — phone is optional on the community form and email alone works. If you do add it, we can text you the moment the Pod ships. Texts are opt-in (the checkbox), and you can reply STOP anytime.

What's actually in the gloss?

Clean, plant-based ingredients you can pronounce — shea butter, squalane, glycerin, vitamin E — and we're sourcing organic wherever a supplier can certify it. No fragrance, no parabens, cruelty-free, and the full list goes on the label in plain English. It doesn't sting either: the fuller look comes from hydration, not irritation.
