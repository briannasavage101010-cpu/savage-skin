/ What it is

What it does.

Two parts. One object. A soft squeeze tube of gloss, and an ivory-and-champagne-gold case it snaps into magnetically. The case clips to your phone, your bag, or your wrist.

Lip gloss goes missing because it lives loose at the bottom of a bag. The Lip Pod doesn't live there — it's worn, like a charm. And when the tube runs out you swap the tube, not the whole thing.

The Pod is the idea. The gloss inside still isn't an afterthought: clean, plant-based ingredients, sourced organic wherever a supplier can certify it, with a glassy, non-sticky finish and no fragrance or mystery filler.

    Rich plant butter that seals and softens — the cushiony balm feel.
